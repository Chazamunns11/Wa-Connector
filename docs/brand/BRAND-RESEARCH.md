# Brand name research

16 Oct 2026. The rules come from a multi-source research run, with each claim checked by 3 independent votes. The shortlist and `.com` checks were run separately: each domain was looked up in Verisign's RDAP registry, where 404 means unregistered, and each name was searched for on the web.

## The rules the name has to follow

1. **No "WhatsApp", "WA", "Whats" or anything similar in the brand, product name or domain.** Meta's WhatsApp brand guidelines forbid the mark, or anything similar to it (including variations, phonetic equivalents and abbreviations), in another company's trademark, product name, second-level domain or username. They also forbid combining it with other names or generic terms. A domain like `whatsappghl.com` or `waconnect.com` is out. ([Meta WhatsApp brand](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/), [Meta company brand](https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/))
2. **No "GHL", "HighLevel" or "GoHighLevel" in the brand.** HighLevel's Marketplace rules ban names that impersonate third-party brands. They recommend publisher-first names such as "`<Your Brand>` for `<Service>`", and require unofficial integrations to show a "not affiliated with or endorsed by" disclaimer. ([HighLevel App Review Guidelines](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/oauth/AppReviewGuidelines))
3. **"WhatsApp" can appear only as a plain descriptor, never as the brand.** The Marketplace listing can be "`<Brand>` for WhatsApp" or "`<Brand>` – WhatsApp integration", with a non-affiliation disclaimer. Meta's guidelines contain no explicit permission even for this, so keep it small and factual.
4. **Ads:** the brand has to lead. Meta's ad policy bars making a Meta brand, WhatsApp included, the most prominent part of the creative, and bars implying endorsement or partnership. ([Meta ad brand usage](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/intellectual-property-infringement/brand-usage))
5. **Platform risk, which the product has to be honest about:** Meta allows WhatsApp automation only through approved channels. Linked-device automation very likely counts as "unapproved", which can lead to warnings, restrictions or bans. The site's FAQ already says this plainly. ([Superchat explainer](https://help.superchat.com/en/articles/531130-what-are-unapproved-automations))

**How competitors handle it:** the ones that follow the rules keep WhatsApp out of the company name. TimelinesAI lists "WhatsApp Integration by TimelinesAI", and AppLevel and GREEN-API do the same. WAGHL and AutoWA embed WhatsApp-derived terms and carry more trademark risk. GHL's native WhatsApp is about $10/month per sub-account plus per-message fees, so a flat per-number price is easy to compare against.

## Shortlist (exact `.com` unregistered on 16 Oct 2026; no same-name software found)

| Name | Domain | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Keelbird** | keelbird.com | A keel keeps a boat steady, which fits the reliability and safety story, and "bird" suggests messages in flight. Distinctive, two syllables, easy to trademark. |
| **Replyferry** | replyferry.com | Says what it does: carries your messages across. Clear in ads, slightly long. |
| **Jettychat** | jettychat.com | Friendly, and the "jetty" metaphor suggests docking WhatsApp into GHL. "chat" is generic, so the trademark strength comes from "Jetty". |
| Quaysend | quaysend.com | Short and unusual. Some people will mispronounce "quay" ("kway" rather than "key"). |
| Wharfchat | wharfchat.com | Same harbour idea as Jettychat, a bit heavier. |
| Replyreef | replyreef.com | Alliterative and memorable; the meaning is loose. |
| Chatkeel | chatkeel.com | Same idea as Keelbird, more literal. |
| Dovereply / Kitereply | dovereply.com / kitereply.com | Soft and friendly. "Reply" undersells the outbound use. |

The 30+ real-word names tried first (Relaybird, Chatbridge, Sendwise, Parley and others) all have their `.com` taken.

## Recommendation, with taglines

1. **Keelbird**: "Keep your GHL workflows. Send them on WhatsApp."
2. **Replyferry**: "Every GHL message, carried over WhatsApp."
3. **Jettychat**: "Dock WhatsApp into GoHighLevel in five minutes."

## Before you commit to one

- **Trademark search.** This check covers only the exact `.com` and an obvious same-name product. Search UKIPO, EUIPO and USPTO in classes 9, 38 and 42 for the chosen name. A trademark attorney's clearance is cheap insurance before spending on ads.
- Register the `.com` straight away, and the `.co.uk` / `.io` if you want them.
- Check the social handles you'll use (Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn).
- Applying the name to the site is one change: set `BRAND_NAME` (plus tagline and colours) in config.
