Straight answers

Will my number get banned?

It can happen. WhatsApp does not approve of automated messaging to people who have not asked for it. The connector paces sends, caps each number daily and honours STOP replies to lower the risk, but it cannot remove it. Message people who expect to hear from you.

Is this the official WhatsApp Business API?

No. It links your existing WhatsApp number as a linked device, the same way WhatsApp Web does. There is no template approval and no per-conversation fee.

What happens if a phone goes offline?

Messages wait while the phone is disconnected and go out once it reconnects. Nothing is dropped silently.

Do I have to rebuild my workflows?

No. You choose the connector as the Telephony Provider and your existing Send SMS actions and conversations carry on.

What if someone replies STOP?

They are opted out and no further messages go to them from your account.

What if a payment fails?

Sending continues while the payment is retried and pauses if the subscription ends unpaid. You can cancel any time from the billing portal.
