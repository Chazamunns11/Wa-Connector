How you set it up

  1. Install the app

    Add PingWren to your GoHighLevel account from the Marketplace.

  2. Set the Telephony Provider

    Choose PingWren as the provider, so “Send SMS” steps go out on WhatsApp.

    Settings → Phone System → Advanced Settings → Telephony Provider

  3. Scan the QR code

    Open WhatsApp on the phone with your business number and link it as a device, the same way you would link WhatsApp Web.

How it treats your number

Paced sending

Messages go out a few at a time with gaps between them, with a daily cap of 150 per number, instead of all at once.

Held while disconnected

If the phone drops the link, nothing is sent from that number. Messages wait, and you see it on the dashboard.

STOP opt-outs

When someone replies STOP, we stop messaging them from that number and note it against their contact.

Read this before you sign up

  • PingWren connects to WhatsApp as a linked device, the way WhatsApp Web does. It is not the official WhatsApp Business API.
  • WhatsApp can restrict or ban a number. Pacing, the daily cap and STOP handling lower that risk. They can’t remove it.
  • The 85%+ figure is our own average across businesses we’ve run WhatsApp appointment reminders for, compared with before PingWren. It is not a study, and your results will vary.

Questions we get

Do I need the WhatsApp Business app or API?

No. You scan a QR code with the phone that already has your number, and PingWren links to it as a device.

Do I have to rebuild my workflows?

No. Once PingWren is the Telephony Provider, the “Send SMS” steps you already have go out on WhatsApp.

Where do replies go?

Back into GoHighLevel Conversations, on the same contact, so your team answers where they already work.

What happens if the phone goes offline?

Sending from that number is held until it reconnects, and the dashboard shows it as disconnected.
