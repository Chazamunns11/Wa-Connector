Why we made it

We run appointment reminders for clinics, vets and gyms in GoHighLevel. The workflows were fine. The text messages were the weak part: people ignore them, or never see them.

So we moved the same "Send SMS" steps onto WhatsApp, on the business's own number, and left everything else in GoHighLevel as it was. Show rate across the businesses we've run this for went from about 50% to 85%+.

That is our own average from before PingWren, not a study. Yours may differ.

How you set it up

Three steps, about ten minutes, no changes to your workflows.

  1. Install the app

    Add PingWren to your GoHighLevel account from the marketplace.

  2. Set the Telephony Provider

    Choose PingWren in your sub-account settings. Your "Send SMS" steps now go out on WhatsApp.

    Settings → Phone System → Advanced Settings → Telephony Provider

  3. Scan the QR code

    On the phone with the business's WhatsApp, open Linked devices and scan. It stays connected like any linked device.

Read this before you sign up

We would rather you know now than find out later.

Linked device
PingWren connects the way WhatsApp Web does. It is not the official WhatsApp Business API.
Restrictions
WhatsApp can restrict or ban a number. Pacing, the daily cap and STOP handling lower that risk. They cannot remove it.
Who it is not for

Questions we get

Do I need to rebuild my workflows?

No. Keep the "Send SMS" steps you already have. Once PingWren is your Telephony Provider, they go out on WhatsApp.

Where do replies go?

Into GoHighLevel Conversations, on the same contact, so your team answers from where they already work.

Can I use it for several sub-accounts?

Yes. Each sub-account connects its own WhatsApp number, and the agency dashboard lists them all.

What happens if the phone goes offline?

The number shows as disconnected and reminders are held. Reconnect from the dashboard and they continue.

How does the free trial work?

Seven days, up to 3 numbers. We take a card up front, and you can cancel from the billing portal whenever you like.

Is this the official WhatsApp Business API?

No. It is a linked device, which is why the disclosures above matter.
