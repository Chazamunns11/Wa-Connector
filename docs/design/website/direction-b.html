Why we made it

We run appointment reminders for clinics, coaches and gyms, and text messages kept getting ignored.

Clients read WhatsApp. So we built the bridge: your existing GoHighLevel workflows keep working exactly as they do now, and the Send SMS step goes out on WhatsApp instead.

Across the businesses we've run WhatsApp appointment reminders for, show rate went from about 50% to 85%+. That is our own average, from before PingWren, and not a study. Results vary.

How you set it up

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Add PingWren to your GoHighLevel account from the marketplace.

  2. 2

    Set the Telephony Provider

    Choose PingWren as your provider.

    Settings → Phone System → Advanced Settings → Telephony Provider
  3. 3

    Scan the QR code

    Open WhatsApp on the phone, go to linked devices and scan. Your number is live.

How it treats your number

  • Paced sendingMessages go out a few seconds apart, with a daily cap of 150 per number, so your number behaves like a person and not a broadcast tool.
  • Held while disconnectedIf the phone drops off WhatsApp, nothing is sent. Messages wait and you see it on the dashboard.
  • STOP opt-outsWhen someone replies STOP, we stop messaging them and flag it for you.

Read this before you sign up

It's a linked device
PingWren connects to WhatsApp the way WhatsApp Web does. It does not use the official WhatsApp Business API.
WhatsApp can restrict a number
WhatsApp can limit or ban a number, and we can't prevent that. Pacing, the daily cap and STOP handling lower the risk. They don't remove it.

Questions we get

Do I need to rebuild my workflows?

No. Your existing workflows stay as they are. Once PingWren is the Telephony Provider, the Send SMS steps go out on WhatsApp.

Which phone do I scan with?

The phone that holds the WhatsApp number you want to send from. It links as a device, like WhatsApp Web.

Where do replies go?

Straight back into GoHighLevel Conversations, on the same contact as before.

What does the trial cost?

Nothing for 7 days, on up to 3 numbers. We take a card up front, and you can cancel from the billing portal whenever you like.

Is this the official WhatsApp Business API?

No. It's a linked device, so please read the section above before you sign up.
