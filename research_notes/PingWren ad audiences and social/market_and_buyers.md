# PingWren: GHL WhatsApp Connector Market, Buyers and Competitors (research as of 17 Oct 2026)

## 1. How big is the GoHighLevel user base, and which countries are largest?

### Takeaway
HighLevel's own figure is about 60,000+ agencies, with "1M+ downstream businesses" in less reliable sources. There is no audited or official 2026 count. On web-traffic proxies the US leads at about 33-38% of traffic. The UK (about 4%), Canada (about 3.5-4%) and Australia (about 4% of app traffic) are the largest English-speaking markets after it. The Philippines, India and Pakistan carry heavy traffic, but much of that is likely VAs and freelancers, not buyers.

### Cited Findings
- Several 2026 reviews give 60,000+ agency customers as of April 2026, but this is the company's own claim. No official press release or audited financials turned up. — [AI Funnel Insider review 2026](https://aifunnelinsider.com/gohighlevel-review-2026/); [Vantaige profile](https://vantaige.io/ai-tool/gohighlevel)
- One sales-oriented profile says "60,000+ agency customers and over 1 million downstream business users". Low reliability. — [Vantaige](https://vantaige.io/ai-tool/gohighlevel)
- A blog says ARR has "reportedly crossed $200M". Unverified. — [Is GoHighLevel Legit? (autogencrm, 2026)](https://autogencrm.com/?p=3950)
- TheirStack detects 12,468 companies using GoHighLevel. Of those with a country: US 1,403, UK 201, Canada 169, India 72, Australia 58, Pakistan 53. Most (84%) have no country attributed. The UK is the second-largest identified country, at about 1/7 of the US count. — [TheirStack](https://theirstack.com/en/technology/gohighlevel)
- Semrush for gohighlevel.com (updated Feb 2026): US about 38%, Philippines about 12%, Pakistan about 4%, UK 3.93%, Canada 3.48%. For app.gohighlevel.com: US about 33%, then the Philippines and India, with Canada at 4.06% and Australia at 3.96%. — [Semrush gohighlevel.com overview](https://semrush.com/website/gohighlevel.com/overview); [Semrush app.gohighlevel.com](https://ja.semrush.com/website/app.gohighlevel.com/overview)
- HighLevel plan prices: Starter $97/mo, Unlimited $297/mo, Agency Pro $497/mo. The SaaS/reselling tier allows reselling WhatsApp to sub-accounts. — [HighLevel pricing](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing); [EIN Presswire plan guide](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/839463862/gohighlevel-pricing-saas-and-white-label-options-detailed-in-updated-plan-guide)
- HighLevel had about 1,930 observed employee profiles as of July 2026, a coverage measure rather than a reported headcount. — [Unify headcount](https://www.unifygtm.com/insights/headcount/highlevel)

### Inferences
- Taking 60k agencies and the 1M+ sub-account claim at face value gives on the order of 15+ sub-accounts per agency on average. Each sub-account is a potential PingWren "number".
- Using the UK share of identified companies (about 1.6% of 12.5k, or about 14% of geo-identified US+UK+CA+IN+AU+PK) and the UK share of traffic (about 4%), a rough UK estimate is 2,000-4,000 GHL agencies and SaaS resellers. This is an order-of-magnitude extrapolation, not a sourced figure.
- The large Philippines, Pakistan and India traffic is mostly GHL VAs, builders and freelancers. They influence tool choice inside agencies (they set up sub-accounts), but they are weak direct buyers.

### Gaps
- No official 2025-2026 HighLevel count of agencies, sub-accounts or SaaS-mode resellers, and no official country breakdown.
- No data on what share of agencies run SaaS mode or reselling.
- Similarweb country data was not retrievable. Semrush and TheirStack are proxies and their numbers disagree.

## 2. Which GHL segments rely most on WhatsApp? (country and vertical evidence)

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is close to universal among internet users in LATAM, India, Southern Africa and Southern Europe (85-97%), and in the UK it is the most-used social or messaging platform (about 81-83%). In the US it is a minority channel, so US GHL agencies (the largest group) are poor WhatsApp prospects except those serving immigrant or international audiences. Competitor testimonials and positioning point to LATAM (Mexico, Venezuela), Israel, Australia and South Asia, plus real estate and coaching niches. No quantitative WhatsApp-adoption data by vertical was found.

### Cited Findings
- UK: WhatsApp is the most active social platform among UK internet users at 82.6% (DataReportal via Statista, Q2 2025). Facebook is at 72.7% and Messenger at 56.3%. — [Statista UK top platforms](https://www.statista.com/statistics/867578/top-active-social-media-platforms-in-the-uk/)
- UK: Sprout Social (2025 data) calls WhatsApp the #1 social platform in the UK, at 81% usage. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/whatsapp-marketing-for-small-business/)
- UK: Analyzify gives a lower figure, 73% of internet users aged 16-64 using it monthly, on a narrower base. — [Analyzify](https://analyzify.com/statsup/whatsapp)
- Estimated WhatsApp share of internet users: South Africa about 95% (94% daily), Brazil about 96%, India about 97% (535M+ users, the largest market), Spain about 85-88%. These are secondary sources modelled from DataReportal/Statista/Meta, and only African figures are survey-backed. — [WeddingKart WhatsApp vs email by country (2026)](https://www.weddingkart.co/blog/whatsapp-vs-email-adoption-by-country); [Yazi Africa penetration](https://www.askyazi.com/articles/whatsapp-penetration-across-africa-statistics-by-country); [Infobip stats 2026](https://www.infobip.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics)
- Usage is highest in Africa and Latin America and among the lowest in Japan. — [Statista penetration by country](https://www.statista.com/statistics/291540/mobile-internet-user-whatsapp-penetration-countries/)
- UK businesses: 80% of firms use apps like WhatsApp or Teams for business, and 46% use them most often to talk to clients (Vodafone Business, Aug 2024, not SME-specific). — [Vodafone press release 23 Aug 2024](https://www.vodafone.co.uk/newscentre/press-release/study-uncovers-importance-online-networks-small-businesses/)
- UK consumers: 1 in 3 Brits would prefer to reach customer service by SMS or WhatsApp (Esendex survey, older and undated). — [365retail](https://365retail.co.uk/one-in-three-brits-would-prefer-to-reach-customer-service-via-whatsapp/)
- GoGHL testimonials come from Mexico, Israel, Australia, Switzerland and Venezuela. An Israeli CEO says it "works perfectly" for the LATAM market "where Meta verification is hard to get", and a Mexican YouTuber credits it for real estate. GoGHL targets real estate, coaching and workshops, and Meta-ad marketers wanting automated follow-up. — [GoGHL.ai](https://goghl.ai/)
- WAGHL is reported as popular in Pakistan and South Asia, and Wazzap targets Latin America. — [OneExpand GHL WhatsApp guide 2026](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-integration/)
- HighLevel itself shipped WhatsApp Coexistence to India in a dedicated changelog, which signals that India is a priority WhatsApp market for HighLevel. — [HighLevel changelog: Coexistence live in India](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/whatsapp-coexistence-live-in-india)

### Inferences
- Ranked international opportunity for an English-language, GBP-priced QR connector (fit = WhatsApp penetration × GHL presence × ability to pay × language):
  1. **UK**: second-largest identified GHL country, 82% WhatsApp reach, home market, and GBP pricing removes FX friction.
  2. **Australia / New Zealand**: about 4% of GHL app traffic and an active GHL agency scene. WhatsApp is less dominant there than in the UK; this is an assumption, since no AU penetration figure was retrieved.
  3. **Ireland + EU English-speaking agencies (Spain/Portugal/NL expat and agency hubs)**: 85%+ WhatsApp reach.
  4. **South Africa**: about 95% WhatsApp reach. Meta Coexistence is *not available* for SA numbers, so the native "keep your app number" path is blocked, which is a strong wedge for QR. Rand pricing sensitivity is a risk.
  5. **UAE / Gulf**: high-value agencies and expat businesses, but no penetration figure was found.
  6. **LATAM, India, Pakistan**: highest WhatsApp dependence, but price-sensitive and already served by cheaper local or Spanish-language QR tools (Stevo in BRL, Wazzap, WAGHL). Lower priority for GBP ads.
  7. **US**: largest GHL base but low WhatsApp reliance. Target only niches serving Hispanic, immigrant or international clients.
- Verticals: WhatsApp-heavy niches inside GHL are those where the owner already sells from a personal or business phone. That means online and fitness coaches (check-ins and sales DMs), real estate (cited by competitors), med spas and aesthetics, and clinics or dental in WhatsApp-first countries. This rests on competitor positioning, not measured data.

### Gaps
- No survey data on WhatsApp usage by vertical (fitness, med spa, dental, solar, real estate) in the UK or elsewhere.
- No source counts GHL users who already use WhatsApp, and no data on how many have enabled native GHL WhatsApp.
- No reliable DataReportal 2026 figures for the UAE, Australia, Ireland or Canada.

## 3. Agencies vs single-location businesses vs coaches: which is the best first ad audience?

### Takeaway
Agencies and SaaS-mode resellers are the best first paid audience. One win brings many numbers, the 10+ volume tier and resale margin are built for them, and every competitor's pricing ladder and affiliate programme is aimed at them. Coaches are a strong second audience, especially in the UK where WhatsApp is the default coaching channel, but each sale is one number.

### Cited Findings
- Competitor pricing is built around agency volume. WAGHL charges $29/mo for a single sub-account and $19/mo per sub-account at 11+. GoGHL charges $29 (1), $22 (3+) and $19 (10+), with white-label and an agency dashboard from 3+. — [WAGHL](https://waghl.com/); [GoGHL](https://goghl.ai/)
- GoGHL claims 65,000+ installs and 3,400+ agencies. That is about 19 installs per agency, which suggests agencies install across many sub-accounts. These are vendor claims, and its own marketplace badge shows 4.7 (28 reviews) against the "4.9, 125+ reviews" claimed elsewhere. — [GoGHL](https://goghl.ai/)
- An app directory lists WAGHL at about 5,500 installs, published Sept 2022. — [AppsForGHL WAGHL listing](https://www.appsforghl.com/apps/waghl-whatsapp)
- HighLevel's native WhatsApp is designed for agency rebilling: a 1x-10x usage markup (default 1.05x) and a per-sub-account customer price set in Agency Settings > Reselling. Agencies are already used to reselling WhatsApp. — [HighLevel WhatsApp Pricing, Billing & Rebilling Guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- One blog notes that when a client buys native WhatsApp, "anything above $10 is your margin". — [Upmish GHL WhatsApp pricing 2026](https://upmish.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-pricing/)

### Inferences
- PingWren is £14.99 (about $19-20) per number, falling to £7.99 (about $10-11) at 10+. That puts the 10+ tier well below WAGHL and GoGHL ($19/sub-account at scale) and close to GHL native's $10 platform fee, with no per-message Meta fees. This is the core agency ad angle: "below native cost, no Meta fees, resell at £30+". At single-number level, £14.99 is cheaper than the $29 entry price of both main competitors.
- A pricing-unit difference matters for messaging. Competitors charge per sub-account (GoGHL bundles 5 numbers, WAGHL 3), while PingWren charges per number. For multi-number single businesses PingWren may look dearer, so ads should stress the per-client resale story.
- Suggested sequencing:
  1. **UK and international GHL agencies or SaaS resellers serving coaches, clinics, med spa and real estate.** Highest LTV per conversion and reachable through GHL-interest targeting, GHL Facebook groups and YouTube.
  2. **UK online and fitness coaches already on GHL or white-label GHL** (e.g. coach-focused SaaS like FitPro-style CRMs). High WhatsApp dependence, simple single-number decision.
  3. **Single-location local businesses**: best reached *through* agencies, not directly.

### Gaps
- No public data on churn, ARPU or conversion for these competitors, so per-segment LTV cannot be validated.
- No data on what share of GHL agencies run SaaS mode.

## 4. Pain points with GHL's native WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API via LeadConnector). Verified current numbers.

### Takeaway
Native GHL WhatsApp costs **$10/month per WhatsApp-enabled sub-account** (not per number), plus Meta per-message fees and a 5% Stripe processing fee. **From 1 Oct 2026, service replies are billable after the first 1,000 per number per month**, which removes the "free replies" argument. UK marketing templates cost $0.0667 and utility $0.0231 per message. Other friction: template approval, the 24-hour window, Meta Business verification, one Coexistence number per account, no groups or calls on Coexistence, and Coexistence unavailable for Nigeria and South Africa.

### Cited Findings
- "$10/month per sub-account" for each WhatsApp-enabled sub-account. Conversation and template charges are on top, by usage. Agencies can rebill with a 1x-10x markup (default 1.05x). A 5% Stripe fee is added to WhatsApp rates. (Article modified 22 Sep 2026.) — [HighLevel WhatsApp Pricing, Billing & Rebilling Guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- **Changes effective 1 Oct 2026:** service messages become billable per message, with the first 1,000 per phone number per month free. In-window utility messages become billable at the utility rate. Free-entry-point (Click-to-WhatsApp) conversations stay free for 72 hours. — [same guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- **UK rates (USD):** marketing $0.0667 per message, utility $0.0231. Service was free through 30 Sep 2026. — [same guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- US rates: $0.0263 marketing and $0.0036 utility (including the 5%). India: $0.0124 marketing and $0.0015 utility. Delivered-message billing since 1 Jul 2025. — [TimelinesAI GHL pricing, checked 1 Sep 2026](https://timelines.ai/gohighlevel-whatsapp-pricing)
- Conflict: one blog says the live wallet shows $0.0250 and $0.0034 for the US because HighLevel removed the 5% fee, but HighLevel's guide still mentions the 5%. — [Upmish](https://upmish.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-pricing/) vs [HighLevel guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- HighLevel's comparison page (modified 15 Oct 2026): $10/mo add-on, 5% Stripe-only fee, no markup, one official WABA per location. — [HighLevel WhatsApp Platform Pricing & Feature Comparison](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007602-whatsapp-platform-pricing-feature-comparison)
- Template rule: marketing, utility and authentication conversations can only be opened with approved templates. The 24-hour service window resets with each customer message. — [HighLevel guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- Third-party list of native friction: Facebook Business Manager verification ("a real friction point"), designing around the 24-hour window, per-message template charges, needing a new or migrated number that can't stay on the WhatsApp app unless Coexistence is used, some countries unsupported, and the template-approval learning curve. — [OneExpand](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-integration/)
- **Coexistence (native "keep your app number"):** the setup scans a QR code from the WhatsApp Business App and imports up to 6 months of history. Only **one Coexistence number per account**. Groups, disappearing and view-once messages, calls and catalogues are unsupported, and linked companion devices are unlinked. Templates can only be sent from the CRM. Nigeria and South Africa numbers are ineligible; India was later added. — [HighLevel: WhatsApp Coexistence](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000003417-whatsapp-coexistence-feature-for-dual-platform-messaging); [changelog: expanded global rollout](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/whatsapp-coexistence-expanded-global-rollout)
- Meta Coexistence also requires a WhatsApp *Business* app number (not personal WhatsApp), and new Business app accounts may not be immediately eligible because of tenure and quality checks. Throughput is 5 mps, with no calling. — [360dialog Coexistence docs](https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/hub/embedded-signup/whatsapp-coexistence); [8x8 Coexistence](https://developer.8x8.com/connect/docs/whatsapp/whatsapp-business-app-coexistence)
- Demand signal: a HighLevel ideas-board post asks HighLevel to add a QR-code WhatsApp connection option. Another argues the $10 WhatsApp fee should be removed. — [Ideas: QR integration improvement](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/whatsapp/p/mejora-para-integracion-de-whatsapp-via-codigo-qr-en-gohighlevel); [Ideas board WhatsApp category](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/whatsapp) (vote counts not retrievable; 403)
- A Spanish-language guide (Aug 2026) says native is $10 per sub-account per month plus per-message charges, and marketing messages are always charged. — [Guía de Software](https://www.guiadesoftware.com/blog/gohighlevel-whatsapp)

### Inferences
- Ad hooks backed by these facts: "Keep your existing WhatsApp (personal or Business) and your groups", "No Meta verification or template approval", "No per-message fees (UK marketing template = 6.7¢ each natively)", "Works on many numbers per client (native Coexistence = 1)", "Works in South Africa/Nigeria where native Coexistence doesn't".
- The 1 Oct 2026 change (service replies billable beyond 1,000 per number) is a timely trigger to re-message agencies about native costs.
- The counter-risk to address in messaging: unofficial and QR connections carry Meta ban risk (see section 5).

### Gaps
- Native GHL WhatsApp adoption rates and complaint volume could not be quantified. Reddit and Facebook-group threads were not retrievable.
- UK service-message rate after 1 Oct 2026 was not given explicitly.

## 5. Competitors: pricing, positioning, acquisition, complaints

### Takeaway
The QR/unofficial GHL WhatsApp category is established and crowded, and its prices cluster around $19-$30 per sub-account per month. GoGHL (claims 3,400+ agencies and 65k installs) and WAGHL (about 5.5k installs, since 2022) lead. Others are WhatsLevel ($30), Wazzap (LATAM), Stevo (Brazil, priced in BRL), TimelinesAI (per-seat, not native inbox) and Zapier hacks. Acquisition is mainly the GHL Marketplace, affiliate programmes (15-20% recurring), free trials, and YouTube and comparison content. The main complaint across the category is ban or restriction risk and session drops.

### Cited Findings
- **GoGHL.ai (MessageSync)**: $29/sub-account (1), $22 (3+), $19 (10+). 5 WhatsApp numbers included per sub-account, then $9/mo per extra number. WhatsApp + iMessage + SMS. Managed iMessage at $250/mo. Built by ex-HighLevel staff, white-label. Claims 65,000+ installs, 3,400+ agencies and 4.9 from 125+ marketplace reviews, while the displayed badge shows 4.7 (28). 15% recurring affiliate. Publishes "vs" comparison pages (e.g. GoGHL vs WaGHL). — [GoGHL.ai](https://goghl.ai/); [GoGHL vs WaGHL](https://goghl.ai/competitor/goghl-vs-waghl); [GoGHL blog](https://goghl.ai/blog/gohighlevel-whatsapp-integration)
- **WAGHL**: Starter $29/mo ($290/yr) for 1 sub-account with up to 3 numbers, then $10 per extra number. Agency Scale $19/mo per sub-account at 11+. 14-day no-card trial plus 50% off for 3 months. One QR scan can serve unlimited sub-accounts. AI replies and voice notes, booking bot, buttons and carousels, SMS fallback. Fully white-label. 20% recurring affiliate. No testimonials on site. — [WAGHL](https://waghl.com/); about 5,500 installs, published Sept 2022 — [AppsForGHL](https://www.appsforghl.com/apps/waghl-whatsapp)
- **WhatsLevel**: about $30 per sub-account, routes through GHL as a custom telephony provider, with more technical setup. **Wazzap**: QR-based, LATAM-focused, unlimited messaging. **Zapier + WhatsApp**: $20+/mo, basic only. — [OneExpand](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-integration/)
- **Stevo.chat**: Brazilian. R$59 for 1 instance (5-day trial), R$129 for 3, R$189 for 5. White-label panel, GHL workflows, offers both official and unofficial APIs. — [Stevo.chat](https://stevo.chat/)
- **GOWA**: Product Hunt launch of a QR WhatsApp plugin for GHL, with "flat pricing, and unlimited messages". Price not listed. — [hunted.space GOWA](https://hunted.space/product/gowa)
- **TimelinesAI**: free GHL Marketplace app, but $35/seat (Starter) or $49/seat (Pro). Each seat connects one WhatsApp number. The inbox stays in TimelinesAI, and GHL gets notes only, not native Conversations. No media sending from GHL workflows. 10-day trial. Acquires through SEO comparison content. — [TimelinesAI GHL pricing](https://timelines.ai/gohighlevel-whatsapp-pricing)
- YouTube comparison content exists ("4 Ways to Integrate WhatsApp with GHL"). — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPBJazOvpJo)
- **Complaints and risk across the category**: unofficial linked-device sessions risk bans, Meta "will not help you with an account you ran through an unofficial client", and sessions drop and need a re-scan after unlinking, inactivity or protocol changes. — [Artificially Intimidating](https://artificiallyintimidating.com/p/go-whatsapp-web-multidevice). Shopify reviewers report restrictions from "link a device" apps ("can get your number banned"). — [Shopify app reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/reviews/1947884). OneExpand flags WAGHL's "higher risk of account restrictions". — [OneExpand](https://oneexpand.com/gohighlevel-whatsapp-integration/). Common ban triggers are bulk sending, user reports, and messaging people who haven't opted in. — [GHL-hosted guide](https://app.gohighlevel.com/v2/preview/noJCzCQJC05Gq1HpwRHS/b/mastering-whatsApp-like-a-pro-how-to-use-and-outsmart-the-system)
- Wapikit, Whapi (as a GHL connector), Chatarmin and Chatwoot bridges: no GHL-specific pricing or listings found. Whapi only appeared as a Shopify app. — [Shopify Whapi](https://apps.shopify.com/reviews/2063974)

### Inferences
- PingWren's differentiators against this field: the only GBP-priced, UK-based option found; the lowest entry price (£14.99, about $19, vs $29); £7.99 at 10+ undercuts the $19/sub-account scale tiers; and ban-mitigating features (STOP/START opt-outs, DND sync, held messages while offline) answer the category's main complaint. Ads should lead on "safer, compliant-by-design QR" and "UK support/GBP billing".
- Expect competitors to bid on GHL WhatsApp comparison keywords and run affiliate-driven YouTube. Matching a 15-20% recurring affiliate offer is table stakes.

### Gaps
- No G2, Capterra or Trustpilot review sets were found for GHL-specific WhatsApp apps, and individual GHL Marketplace reviews were not retrievable.
- No data on competitors' paid-ad spend or Facebook-group activity.
- "Wapikit", "WhatsApp for GHL" as a distinct brand, Chatarmin and Chatwoot bridges could not be confirmed as active GHL competitors.

## 6. Buyer triggers and moments

### Takeaway
The strongest documented triggers are native-setup friction moments: Meta verification failures, a client refusing to move their number off the WhatsApp app, Coexistence limits (one number, no groups), Coexistence being unavailable in South Africa and Nigeria, and Meta's 1 Oct 2026 billing change. Agency moments are onboarding a new client sub-account and migrating clients from other CRMs into GHL.

### Cited Findings
- HighLevel's redesigned onboarding offers "Connect, Create, or Migrate" paths. Each is a decision point where the agency weighs native against a QR app. — [HighLevel changelog: new WhatsApp onboarding flow](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/new-whatsapp-onboarding-flow-connect-create-or-migrate); [Redesigned onboarding](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008043-redesigned-whatsapp-onboarding-experience-in-highlevel)
- HighLevel's "Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?" and "migrate to Cloud API" help articles exist because number migration is a common question. — [Existing number FAQ](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006748-can-i-use-my-existing-whatsapp-number-for-registration-in-gohighlevel); [Migration article](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007590-how-to-migrate-your-existing-whatsapp-account-to-whatsapp-cloud-api)
- GoGHL testimonial: LATAM users choose QR because "Meta verification is hard to get". — [GoGHL](https://goghl.ai/)
- Billing change on 1 Oct 2026: service replies are billable beyond 1,000 per number. — [HighLevel guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide)
- Users are asking on the ideas board for QR connection and removal of the $10 fee. — [Ideas QR post](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/whatsapp/p/mejora-para-integracion-de-whatsapp-via-codigo-qr-en-gohighlevel)

### Inferences
- Ad targeting moments: (a) agencies newly onboarding WhatsApp-first clients, such as coaches or clinics who "run the business from WhatsApp"; (b) migrations from other CRMs (e.g. a coach moving from a WhatsApp-native tool into GHL, matching the ghl-account-migration workflow); (c) people searching "GHL WhatsApp without Meta verification", "keep WhatsApp number GHL" or "GHL WhatsApp groups"; (d) retargeting visitors to HighLevel WhatsApp help docs isn't possible directly, but those queries are good keyword targets.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/gohighlevel or GHL Facebook-group threads were retrieved, so the volume and wording of complaints are unverified. Manual review of those communities is recommended before final ad copy.
